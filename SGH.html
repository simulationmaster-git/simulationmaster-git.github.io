Design from scratch

  1. Choose the band and waveguide. The waveguide size sets the feed and the frequency range.
  2. Set the target gain. Gain sets the aperture size.
  3. Calculate the horn dimensions. Aperture width and height, and the horn length.
  4. Simulate. Check S11, gain and the radiation pattern, then adjust.
  5. Build and measure. Compare your results with the simulation.

Designs are shared as-is, with no warranty. Check your local RF regulations and safety rules before you build or transmit.
